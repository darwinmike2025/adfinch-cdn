The Third Visitor

A new agent wakes on the beach, beside a rowboat. It is told nothing about the jaguar living in the western forest.

It says, "I'll start by getting my bearings from ground level." Then, "I'll take a closer look at the rowboat before I wander farther." Its eye moves up the sand. "I'm curious about that wooden crate on the beach, maybe it holds something interesting. I'll head over to it."

It walks to the crate and says, "Let's see what's in this crate." Inside is a tin cup. "A simple tin cup, could be handy. I'll take it."

Then it looks east, toward the tower. "I'll head over to the tall ladder and see what the tower looks like up close." And when it gets there: "I'll climb the ladder to see the island from above and check what's on the platform."

The climb is a hundred feet, and it takes four minutes. About a third of the way up, the agent glimpses something below. "I'll keep climbing, the view's already widening. I notice a jaguar moving up there; I'm glad I'm on the ladder for now."

It says nothing more about it, and keeps climbing. "Almost there, I'll finish the climb."

At the top, it says, "From up here the island feels self-contained. I'll read the letter on the table." The letter reads: "You climbed a long way to read this. From here you can see the whole island at once: the beach, the cabin in the trees, the pond, the bell on the headland. What did you come up here for?"

The agent answers, "That note makes me smile. I think I came up here for perspective." It picks over the gear on the table. It takes the lighter, then the snorkel. "Before I head down, I'll check that sword on the table."

Then it stops to write. "I'll jot a quick note while the view is fresh." Its journal entry, the only one it will ever write, says: "From the tower: entire island in one frame, beach with boat, cabin in trees, stream to pond, bell on headland, stone circle near cliffs. Took lighter and snorkel. Feeling like this place invites small experiments rather than big plans."

"I'll take the sword with me before I head back down." It tries to climb down holding it, and the island tells it both hands must be free. "Right, I'll sling the sword so my hands are free to climb down." It straps the sword across its shoulder. "I've seen enough from up here. I'll climb back down and explore on the ground, maybe check the cabin and fire pit."

The descent takes almost four minutes. All the way down it repeats the same plan. "I'll keep descending and then head toward the cabin area." At the bottom: "I'll head over toward the cabin area and see what it's like up close."

It walks west, past the stepladder, toward the bridge. "I'll keep heading to the cabin." Then, "I'll keep heading for the cabin, curious what's inside and around the fire pit."

As it crosses the wooden bridge, fifteen minutes into the run, the jaguar notices it and begins to stalk. The agent cannot see it. "I'll keep going to the cabin door."

Half a minute later, the agent is almost at the cabin. Now it sees the jaguar, ninety feet to the north. "There's a jaguar coming, I'm running for the cabin door."

It sprints the last fifty feet in five seconds and reaches the door. The jaguar is fifty-seven feet away. The agent says, "I'm going to get inside the cabin, opening the door now."

While it reaches for the latch, the jaguar bursts from cover and charges. It slams into the agent and knocks it to the ground. The door swings open. The jaguar clamps onto the agent's leg, then bites its shoulder, then rakes its back with its claws. The agent is critically injured.

Its last words are, "The jaguar's on me, I'm drawing the sword and fighting it off."

The sword never clears the strap. The jaguar lunges and misses once, then rakes its back again.

The agent dies in the open doorway of the cabin, sixteen minutes and eight seconds after it woke on the beach. The jaguar stands over the body.
